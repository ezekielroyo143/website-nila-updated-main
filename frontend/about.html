OUR STORY

Comfort, convenience, and memorable stays.

Mac Van Mae Resort is designed to give guests a relaxing place to stay while enjoying comfortable accommodations and useful resort amenities.

Located in Patar, our resort is a place where guests can enjoy the beach and pool without an entrance fee. Whether you're staying with family or friends, we want you to have a comfortable and relaxing experience.

Our reservation system makes it easier for guests to browse rooms, create an account, and submit reservations online.
